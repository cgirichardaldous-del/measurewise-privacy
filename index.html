MeasureWise

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-14

Applies to: MeasureWise (Android and iOS), all versions from V1 onward.

This is the canonical, authoritative privacy policy for MeasureWise. Every other place this policy's substance appears (the in-app Privacy Policy screen, the Google Play Data safety form, the Apple App Privacy disclosure, and the public hosted page) must match this document exactly. If any of those ever appears to say something different, this document is the source of truth and the other one is wrong and needs to be fixed.

1. What MeasureWise collects

Nothing. MeasureWise does not collect any information about you, your device, or your use of the app, and transmits nothing to any server anywhere. MeasureWise has no backend, no account system, and makes no network requests for any of its core features.

2. What MeasureWise does NOT collect

To be specific and leave nothing ambiguous, MeasureWise does not collect, and has no code capable of collecting:

- Your name, email address, phone number, or any contact information.

- Your location (no location permission is requested or used).

- Photos, camera, or microphone access (no such permission is requested).

- Contacts, calendar, or files on your device.

- An advertising identifier, device identifier, or any other identifier used to recognize you across apps or over time.

- Analytics or usage statistics of any kind. No analytics software development kit (SDK) is included in the app.

- Crash reports or diagnostic data. No crash-reporting SDK is included in the app.

- Payment or financial information. MeasureWise has no purchases, subscriptions, or billing of any kind.

- Anything you type into the app's amount, ingredient, or Recipe Scaler fields, in the sense of this section: none of it is ever transmitted anywhere. (Some of it - the amount and units/ingredient you use in a normal conversion - IS saved on your own device as local recent-conversion history, if "Remember history" is on; section 3 below covers exactly what's stored locally and how to clear it. Recipe Scaler is the one exception that is never stored at all, not even locally.)

3. What data is stored locally, on your own device

MeasureWise stores a small amount of information, but only on your own device, in the app's own private storage area (never on a server, because no server exists):

- App preferences: your appearance choice (system/light/dark), whether Kitchen Lock is on, whether history is remembered, your result style (Kitchen Friendly or Precise), and which measuring tools you've selected for "Make This Measurable."

- Favorite conversions: the unit pairs you've starred, so you can get back to them quickly.

- Recent conversions: a short history of recent conversions (up to 25), including the amount you typed and the units/ingredient you selected, recorded only while the "Remember history" setting is on. Turning that setting off stops NEW entries from being recorded, but does not delete entries already stored - see section 11 for how to clear them.

- Recent ingredients: a short list of ingredients you've recently used for ingredient-based conversions (up to 5), recorded only while "Remember history" is on, with the same behavior described above: turning the setting off does not delete what's already stored.

Recipe Scaler is the one exception - it stores nothing at all. Whatever you type into the Recipe Scaler (servings, ingredient rows) exists only in the app's temporary memory while that screen is open, and is gone the instant you leave the screen. It is never written to your device's storage and never persisted between sessions.

4. Why local data is stored

Purely for your convenience, so the app remembers your preferences and recent activity the next time you open it, the same way any offline app remembers its settings. None of it is used for any purpose beyond making the app work the way you left it.

5. Whether data leaves your device

No. Never. MeasureWise makes no network requests as part of its normal operation - it works the same with WiFi and cellular data both turned off. There is no server for your data to be sent to, so there is nothing to intercept, no account to be breached remotely, and no data broker relationship of any kind. The INTERNET permission present in the Android app is a leftover of the standard Expo/React Native build tooling template and is not exercised by any feature of the app; MeasureWise's own code makes no use of it.

6. Third-party services actually present

None. MeasureWise includes no third-party analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, no cloud-storage SDK, and no authentication SDK. The app's dependencies (listed in its public source, if shared) are limited to the Expo/React Native framework itself and its standard supporting libraries (navigation, local storage, safe-area handling, vector icons), none of which transmit data anywhere on MeasureWise's behalf.

7. Advertising and analytics status

MeasureWise shows no advertising of any kind and includes no advertising SDK. MeasureWise performs no analytics or usage tracking of any kind and includes no analytics SDK.

8. Account status

MeasureWise requires no account, login, or sign-up of any kind. There is no username, password, or user profile anywhere in the app. Every feature is available immediately on first launch.

9. Children's privacy

MeasureWise does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information from anyone at all - the app's data practices described above apply identically regardless of the user's age. MeasureWise is not directed specifically at children and is a general-purpose kitchen measurement tool suitable for a general audience.

10. Data retention

Since MeasureWise stores data only on your own device and transmits nothing to a server, there is no server-side retention period to disclose - there is no server holding any copy of your data. Locally stored data remains on your device until you clear it yourself (see the next section) or until the app is uninstalled, at which point the operating system removes the app's local storage as part of normal uninstall behavior.

11. How to delete locally stored data

In the app: More -> Clear history removes your recent conversions and recent ingredients - this is the control that actually deletes them. Turning off "Remember history" only stops NEW entries from being recorded; it does not, by itself, delete conversions or ingredients already stored - use Clear history for that. Individual favorites can be removed from the Saved screen.

Uninstalling the app removes all of MeasureWise's local data from your device, per your operating system's standard app-removal behavior.

Because there is no server-side copy of your data, there is nothing further to request deletion of beyond the device-level steps above - MeasureWise cannot "delete your account data from our servers" because no such server-side data exists to delete.

One platform-specific note: the local-storage library MeasureWise uses on iOS is written to exclude its data from your device's backups by default, and MeasureWise has not overridden that default. This has been confirmed by reading that library's source code, but has not yet been separately confirmed against an actual built iOS app, since MeasureWise has not yet had a real iOS build produced (this app is developed on Windows; iOS builds require Apple's own cloud build service, which has not yet been used) - this note will be updated once that direct confirmation is possible. Either way, any backup your device does make is one you control through your own Apple ID and device settings, not a transmission to MeasureWise or anyone else - MeasureWise has no access to your backups and does not create or manage them. On Android, MeasureWise explicitly disables app-data backup (android.allowBackup: false), which does not depend on any third-party library default.

12. Security

MeasureWise stores its local data using the standard, OS-provided private-storage mechanism every app uses on your device, which is protected by whatever device-level security you have set up (device passcode/biometric lock, disk encryption, etc.). We do not claim MeasureWise's local storage is invulnerable to every possible attack - no software can honestly make that claim - only that MeasureWise does not introduce any additional risk of its own: it has no server to be breached, no account credentials to be stolen, and no network transmission to be intercepted. Your device's own security is the practical boundary of what protects this data, the same as for any other app you use.

13. Policy updates

If this policy ever changes, the effective date above will be updated, and the updated policy will be included with the app version that ships alongside the change - you will always be able to view the current policy in the app itself (More -> About -> Privacy Policy), without needing an internet connection.
